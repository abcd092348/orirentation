ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY

Engineering knowledge. Building tomorrow.

The University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila, is a public-sector engineering university in Taxila, Pakistan. It provides an academic environment for engineering, technology and technical learning.

This student-created orientation website introduces new learners to the Robotics and Artificial Intelligence field, important technical concepts and the opportunities available through continued learning and practice.
